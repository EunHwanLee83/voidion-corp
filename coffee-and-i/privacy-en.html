1. Personal Information We Collect and How We Collect It

Voidion (the "Company") handles the personal information of people who use Coffee & I (the "Service") safely, in accordance with the Personal Information Protection Act (PIPA) of Korea and other applicable laws. The Service can be used with an anonymous account without signing up. We process only the information that is truly necessary, and most records are stored only on your phone.

  • Continue with Toss is an optional feature. You can use the other features of the Service without it, and if you do not use it, the Toss identifier and backup are not collected.
  • The Toss login consent screen shows name as a required item, but the Service does not read or store the name that Toss passes on. The Company uses only the member identifier (userKey) needed to identify your account, and does not request your email, phone number, date of birth, gender, connecting information (CI), or similar.
  • Not included in the backup — Cafe photos and treats (group coffee orders) that have not yet been finalized are not backed up.
  • The Service does not collect real names, resident registration numbers, phone numbers, addresses, email addresses, or location information. (The Company receives an email address only if you send us an inquiry email yourself.)
  • We do not create passwords. Your account is identified only by your phone's device key and (if you choose) Toss login.
  • Your name (nickname), avatar and decorations, usual menu (including menu names you type yourself) and memo, treat responses, and who-bought records are visible to members of the same team. Please do not enter your real name or any other information you do not want to share.
  • Stored only on your phone — Coffee and caffeine records, bedtime and caffeine speed settings, cafe stamp book, cafes and cafe photos (photos you choose from your phone's photo album are shrunk to small images and kept only on your phone), your own custom coffees, and reports are not sent to the server, and the Company does not collect them. However, as shown in the table above, if you turn on Continue with Toss, these records (except cafe photos) are stored on the server as a backup.
  • Ads (Toss version) — Ads in the Service when used inside Toss are rewarded full-screen ads provided by Toss (Viva Republica Inc.), and they play only if you choose to watch them. The Service receives only whether you watched the ad to the end. Information processing for providing ads follows Toss's policies.
  • Ads (App Store and Google Play apps) — Rewarded ads in the app downloaded from the stores are provided by LevelPlay, the ad mediation service of Unity Technologies, and the ad networks connected to it (such as Unity Ads). They play only if you choose to watch an ad in the shop. To load and show ads, the ad providers directly collect, through the ad module (SDK) included in the app, the advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS — only if you choose "Allow tracking" / Advertising ID on Android), the identifier for vendors (IDFV), device model, operating system, language and country, IP address (from which an approximate location can be estimated), and records of ad impressions, clicks and completed views. The Company does not receive or store this information and receives only whether you watched the ad to the end. Processing by ad providers follows each provider's privacy policy (Unity). The Service's health, sleep and coffee records are not used for ads.
  • Connection information — To deliver server requests, our cloud provider (Cloudflare, Inc.) processes connection information (such as IP addresses), but the Service itself does not store IP addresses.

Device Permissions

  • Read photo album — An optional permission requested only when you choose a cafe photo. The chosen photo is shrunk to a small image and kept only on your phone; it is not sent to the server. If you do not allow it, you can still use all features other than cafe photos.
  • Write to clipboard — Used only when you copy a team code or order details. We do not read the contents of your clipboard.
  • App tracking permission (iOS app) — An optional permission that asks whether ad providers may use the advertising identifier (IDFA). You can use all features even if you do not allow it.
  • Camera (iOS app) — An optional permission requested only when you take a cafe photo yourself. The photo you take is shrunk to a small image and kept only on your phone.

Installation, Operation and Refusal of Automatic Data Collection Tools

  • App — The Service does not use cookies. Your records are kept in your phone's storage (local storage), and they are deleted when you delete this Service's stored data on your phone or delete the app. The Service (the Company) does not directly read or store advertising identifiers. Ad processing in the Toss version follows Toss's policies, and you can refuse it by not choosing to watch ads.
  • Advertising identifier (App Store and Google Play apps) — The iOS app shows the App Tracking Transparency permission prompt the first time you open the shop, and ad providers use the advertising identifier (IDFA) only if you allow it. Even if you do not allow it, you can watch ads and use other features in the same way (ads unrelated to your interests may be shown), and you can change this later in your phone's Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. In the Android app, you can delete or reset your Advertising ID in your phone's Settings → Privacy (or Google) → Ads. If you do not choose to watch ads, the ad module does not collect information for ads.
  • Web pages — The Company's information web pages, including this policy, do not use cookies or visitor analytics tools.

2. Processing and Retention Period of Personal Information

  • Personal information is kept for as long as the Service is provided, and is destroyed without delay when you request deletion or when the retention period below ends.
  • Server records deleted automatically — The following records are deleted automatically every day at 3 a.m. (Korea time) once their retention period has passed.
    RecordRetention period
    Who-bought record31 days
    Treats (group coffee orders) and each member's response3 days
    Star Candy grant records already delivered60 days
  • When you delete it yourself
    • If you tap "Delete my info from the server" at the very bottom of the My Profile window (opened by tapping your avatar), your information stored on the server is deleted immediately and you are removed from all teams. If you are using Continue with Toss, the Toss connection is also released through Toss's API.
    • If you tap "Disconnect Toss" at the very bottom of the notifications (bell icon) window at the top right of the "Today" tab, your Toss user identifier (userKey) and server backup are deleted immediately. In this case, your team memberships and the records on your phone remain as they are.
    • If you disconnected from within the Toss app, you can request deletion through "Disconnect Toss" in the app or by email as described in Section 7, and it will be deleted immediately in the same way.
    • Star Candy and decorations disappear when you delete your information and cannot be restored.
  • Other server information (the transformed value of the device key, profile, team information, etc.) is kept until you delete it as described above. If a user who has turned on Continue with Toss loads a backup on another phone, the previous anonymous account on that phone is deleted.
  • Long-inactive accounts — Server information for accounts that have not been accessed for 1 year or more may be deleted or stored separately after notice is given at least 30 days in advance through an in-service notice or the Company's web page, in accordance with Article 10 of the Terms of Service. This is based on the last access time recorded on the server.
  • Inquiry records — The content of inquiries you send by email and the reply email address are kept for 3 years as records of consumer complaint and dispute handling, and then destroyed (Act on the Consumer Protection in Electronic Commerce, Etc.).
  • Records stored only on your phone are not held by the Company, and they disappear when you delete the app or delete the app's data on your phone.
  • Retention required by law — Because the Service does not receive payment information and does not store access logs (IP), the only item currently kept separately under the law is inquiry records (3 years, as above). If any information must be kept under the law in the future, it will be stored separately from other information and destroyed without delay when that period ends.

3. Provision to Third Parties, Outsourcing of Processing and Overseas Transfer

Provision to Third Parties

  • The Company does not provide your personal information to third parties (except as required by law). However, as part of how the Service works, your name (nickname), avatar and decorations, usual menu (including menu names you type yourself) and memo, treat responses, and who-bought records are visible to the members of teams you have joined. This is a basic feature of the Service that you use by joining a team, and you can stop it with "Delete my info from the server".
  • Toss (Viva Republica Inc.) is the login and advertising platform on which the Service is offered. The Company receives the user identifier (userKey) from Toss login and receives only whether an ad was fully watched from Toss ads. To process connecting and disconnecting Toss login, the Company calls Toss's API using a certificate issued by Toss (mTLS). Information that Toss processes while providing its own services follows Toss's privacy policy; this is not an outsourcing of personal information processing to Toss or an overseas transfer by the Company.
  • Ad providers (App Store and Google Play apps) — If you choose to watch an ad, the ad providers directly collect the advertising information described in Section 1 through the app's ad module and process it overseas. The Company does not receive this information.

4. Your Rights and How to Exercise Them

  • You may at any time request access to, correction of, deletion of, or suspension of processing of your personal information, and you may withdraw your consent.
  • You can do this yourself in the app — You can edit your profile (name (nickname), avatar, usual menu, memo) directly in the app, delete your server information immediately with "Delete my info from the server" at the very bottom of the My Profile window, and delete your Toss connection and backup immediately with "Disconnect Toss" in the notifications (bell icon) window.
  • You can also make a request by email (info@voidion.kr) or by phone. Because the Service does not identify users by name or contact details and distinguishes them only by the transformed value of the device key, it may be difficult to verify your identity by email or phone alone. In that case, we may guide you to do it yourself in the app, or ask for information needed for verification, such as your team name or name (nickname). After verifying your identity, the Company will take action within 10 days of receiving the request and inform you of the result. If there is a legitimate reason we cannot act within that period, we will inform you of the reason and the schedule for handling it.
  • You may exercise your rights through a representative, such as your legal guardian or a person you have authorized. In this case, a power of attorney in accordance with the Notice on Personal Information Processing Methods must be submitted.
  • If we refuse all or part of a request in accordance with the law, we will inform you of the reason and how to file an objection.
  • Records stored only on your phone cannot be accessed by the Company, so you can view, edit and delete them yourself in the app.

5. Procedure and Method for Destroying Personal Information, and Security Measures

Destruction Procedure

Personal information whose retention period has ended or whose processing purpose has been achieved is destroyed without delay. It is destroyed by the automatic deletion every day at 3 a.m. (Korea time) and by your deletion requests ("Delete my info from the server" and "Disconnect Toss"). Information that must be kept under applicable laws is stored separately from other personal information and destroyed without delay when that period ends.

Destruction Method

Information in electronic file form is deleted in a way that cannot be recovered or reproduced. Deleted server information cannot be restored.

Security Measures

  • Administrative measures — The Chief Privacy Officer handles processing personally, and access to personal information is limited to the Chief Privacy Officer alone.
  • Technical measures — All communication is encrypted (HTTPS); the server stores only a transformed value (hash) of the device key instead of the original value; and we neither receive nor store passwords. Communication with Toss uses only a certificate issued by Toss (mTLS). Access rights to the server and management tools are restricted.
  • Physical measures — We have no in-house server room; data is stored in the secure facilities of our cloud provider (Cloudflare, Inc.).

6. Protection of Children's Personal Information

  • The Service is available to people aged 14 or older (Article 6 of the Terms of Service). This is based on the age at which a person can give consent to the collection and use of personal information on their own.
  • Children under 14 cannot use the Service. The Company does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 14 and does not operate a procedure for verifying a legal guardian's consent. Because the Service does not receive real names, contact details or dates of birth, it cannot directly verify users' ages; by agreeing to this policy and the Terms of Service, users are deemed to have confirmed that they are 14 or older.
  • If we learn that information belongs to a child under 14, we will terminate the service agreement for that account and destroy without delay the personal information stored on the server. A legal guardian can request deletion using the contact details in Section 7.
  • Contractual matters regarding use by minors follow the Terms of Service.
